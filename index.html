About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at the University of Virginia. Firmware and digital design are what I keep coming back to, mostly because I like how far down the stack you can go before you hit physics. So far that has meant an 8-bit processor built and verified in VHDL, a retrieval-augmented search app called Blackhawk AI running on Flask, Supabase, and pgvector, and an analog boost pedal that went from a breadboard to a fabricated two-layer board.

Outside of engineering I lead and mentor with Younglife.
